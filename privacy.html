Privacy policy (politique de confidentialité)

This site is a personal portfolio. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no cookie.

Data controller

Edgar Gaillard, edg.gaillard@gmail.com.

Contact form

The form does not send anything to a server. It opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Your name, email and message are only received if you choose to send that email. I use them to answer you and delete them when they are no longer needed for an internship or project discussion (at most 2 years).

Stored in your browser

If you change the theme (Auto, Light, Dark), your choice is saved in your browser (localStorage, key theme). It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts (Google): loads the typefaces, which sends your IP address to Google.
  • jsDelivr: loads the technology logos in the Skills section.
  • YouTube: nothing is loaded from YouTube on this site. The EVA 1v1 trailer opens on youtube.com only if you click its link.
